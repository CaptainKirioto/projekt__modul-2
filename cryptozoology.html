Cryptozoology

Cryptozoology is the study of creatures whose existence has not been scientifically confirmed. These creatures are often referred to as cryptids.

Some of the most famous examples include Bigfoot, the Loch Ness Monster and the Yeti. Stories about these creatures can be found in folklore and legends around the world.

Cryptozoologists investigate eyewitness accounts, photographs and other possible evidence in an attempt to discover unknown species. However, cryptozoology is generally considered a pseudoscience because its claims often lack reliable scientific evidence.
